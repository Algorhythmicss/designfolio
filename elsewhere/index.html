Getting there & getting home

Shuttles from the Majestic pickup point every 30 minutes, 1 pm–5 pm. Return shuttles 8 pm–10:30 pm. Included with your pass.

Gate 1 for entry. A signed pickup area for taxis. No public parking inside.

Find your corner

The Courtyard. Open sky, live music and space to move. Step-free viewing to the right of the stage.

The Garden. Shared tables, installations and the orange door. Water and a quiet room beside the main path.

The Shed. Covered live electronics and a closing dance. Level access from the garden; earplugs at the door.

Access, comfort & quiet

Step-free routes connect all three spaces. Accessible toilets, seated viewing, free water refills and a quiet room beside the garden.

Access desk at Gate 1. First aid and earplugs at the help point. The team can help you find the listening room.

Coming on your own?

Join an optional 3 pm welcome walk from the garden. Shared tables and open workshops make it easy to meet people. Or simply follow your own plan.

What’s included & what to bring

Every pass includes scheduled music, installations, open workshops and the shuttle. Food, drinks and merchandise cost extra.

Bring photo ID, your ticket and an empty refillable bottle. This is an 18+ event. Small bags welcome; no outside alcohol or professional recording equipment.

Rain & re-entry

Covered spaces stay open in light rain. Severe weather updates appear here. Re-entry with your wristband until 8 pm.

Venue and operating details are imagined for this design study.

INDEPENDENT DESIGN STUDY

A festival imagined.

Elsewhere is a fictional music and arts festival created as a design portfolio project. Artists, venue, dates, prices and operating arrangements are sample content.

The city scenes use original generated artwork inspired by printed photomontage and archival illustration. The blue doorway and the open place index use custom vector compositions inspired by experimental theatre posters and printed graphic art. The In Pictures section contains AI-generated photographic scenes of the imagined courtyard, garden, print workshop and shed. All imagery is conceptual; none documents a real event.

The checkout is a demonstration. No payment, reservation or email is sent.
